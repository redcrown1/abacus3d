# Eclipse Warden

A ritual-summoned boss fight for Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric). Requires Fabric Loader and Fabric API.

## Build

```
./gradlew build
```

Needs Java 21. The mod jar is written to `build/libs/eclipse-warden-1.0.0.jar`; drop it into `mods/`.

## The ritual

1. Craft an **Eclipse Altar** (crying obsidian, gold ingot, crying obsidian / obsidian, eye of ender, obsidian / three obsidian).
2. Place four **lit candles** around it, one on each side, two blocks from the altar.
3. Craft an **Eclipse Sigil** (eye of ender ringed by amethyst shards, gold nuggets in the corners) and use it on the altar.

After a six-second ritual everyone nearby is taken to the **Eclipsed Court**, where the Warden rises.

## The fight

The Warden has 320 health and three phases, at two thirds and one third health. Each phase darkens the sky and adds attacks:
blade slashes and homing bolts from the start, starfall from phase two, sweeping rays in phase three, with shockwave slams and
blinks throughout. The arena cannot be broken or built on in survival.

## Rewards

- **Eclipse Blade** – the Warden's own sword; right-click throws a slash wave.
- **Eclipse Core** and **Umbral Shards** – crafted into the **Warden's Crown** (Night Vision while worn) and the
  **Umbral Scepter** (fires homing bolts).
- 500 experience and two advancements.

When the Warden falls, an altar appears in the centre of the Court; use it to return to where you started.

## Operator commands

- `/eclipsewarden enter` – go to the Court and start the fight
- `/eclipsewarden leave` – return home
- `/eclipsewarden rebuild` – rebuild the arena

## Regenerating assets

Textures, models and data files are generated by `tools/gen_assets.py` and `tools/gen_data.py` (Python 3 with Pillow).
